A famous sour and fiery noodle dish from Rakhine state in western Myanmar, often called "Ar-pu Shar-pu" (hot throat, hot tongue). It can be enjoyed either as a soup noodle or as a dry salad (thote).
<!-- more -->
1. **Prepare the soup base:** Place the fish in a large pot with 1.5 litres of water and 1/4 tsp ground turmeric. Bring to a boil and simmer for 6–10 minutes until the fish is just cooked through.
2. Remove the fish from the pan. Once cool enough to handle, peel off skin and flake the flesh, removing any bones. 
3. Squeeze the flaked fish through a fine sieve or muslin cloth to extract all juices. Pour the fish juice back into the soup pot.
4. Add sliced galangal, crushed garlic, ground black pepper, and shrimp paste to the soup base. Cover with a lid and simmer gently for 10 minutes. Season with salt to taste.
5. **Cook the flaked fish:** In a wok, heat 2 tbsp peanut oil and stir in 1/4 tsp turmeric until vibrantly yellow. Add the flaked fish and stir-fry over low heat for 10 minutes until dried and flaky. Season with 1/2 tsp salt and set aside in a bowl.
6. **Prepare the chili sauce:** Heat 2 tbsp peanut oil in a small pan, add crushed dried chillies, water, and fish sauce. Simmer for about 2 minutes until liquid reduces. Set aside.
7. **Prepare tamarind juice & garnishes:** Simmer tamarind pulp and 250ml water in a small pan for 2 minutes. Strain through a sieve to discard stones and fibers. Prepare the crispy shallots, soak sliced raw shallot in cold water, cook rice noodles according to package instructions, and chop the coriander.
8. **To serve as Soup:** Place rice noodles in a bowl, add a generous portion of stir-fried flaked fish, ladle over the hot soup, and top with chili sauce, tamarind juice, crispy shallots, fresh coriander, and extra pepper to taste.
9. **To serve as Dry Salad (Thote):** Toss rice noodles with flaked fish, tamarind juice, chili sauce, raw/crispy shallots, coriander, and 1 tbsp of shallot-infused oil. Serve with a bowl of the galangal fish soup on the side!

::: slot ingredients
### For the Soup Base & Fish
- Catfish (or brown trout) -- 300g
- Ground turmeric -- 1/2 tsp (divided into two 1/4 tsp portions)
- Water -- 1.5 litres
- Fresh galangal (peeled and sliced) -- 2cm piece
- Garlic (crushed) -- 3 cloves
- Black peppercorns (freshly ground) -- 1 tsp
- Shrimp paste -- 1/2 tsp
- Peanut oil -- 2 tbsp
- Salt -- 1/2 tsp (plus more for soup)

### For the Sauces & Condiments
- Chili Sauce
    - Crushed dried chillies -- 3 tbsp
    - Peanut oil -- 2 tbsp
    - Water -- 3 tbsp
    - Fish sauce -- 2 tbsp
- Tamarind Juice
    - Tamarind pulp -- 45g
    - Water -- 250 ml

### For Assembly & Garnishes
- Rice noodles (cooked) -- 500g
- Shallots -- 2 (made into crispy fried shallots)
- Shallot -- 1 (thinly sliced lengthways & soaked in cold water)
- Fresh coriander (chopped) -- 1 large handful
- Extra fish sauce & freshly ground black pepper (for serving)
