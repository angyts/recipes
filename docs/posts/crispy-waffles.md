For the yummy crispy waffles.
<!-- more -->
1. Sift together the dry ingredients.
2. Separate the eggs.
3. Whisk buttermilk, vanilla and 2 egg yolks.
4. Mix all above ingredients until 1/2 combined. Pour in melted butter and oil until all combined.
5. In a separate bowl, use whisk to beat the egg whites until stiff.
6. Fold them into the batter, stopping short of mixing them all the way.
7. Scoop into the waffles machine and enjoy!

Reference: https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/the-best-waffles-ever-recipe
:::

::: slot ingredients
- Large eggs, separated to yolk and white -- 2
- Buttermilk (sometimes I replace with full cream milk) -- 1.5 cups or 340g
- Unsalted butter -- 2 tbsp or 28g
- Vegetable oil -- 2 tbsp or 25g
- Vanilla extract -- 1/2 tsp
- Flour -- 1 cups or 120g
- Corn starch -- 4 tsp
- Baking powder -- 1 tsp
- Baking soda -- 1/2 tsp
- Salt -- 1/4 tsp
- Sugar -- 1/2 tsp
- (if using sourdough discard: 1/2 cup or 120g sourdough, 1/2 cup or 60g flour, 1 1/4 cups or 280g buttermilk)
:::
