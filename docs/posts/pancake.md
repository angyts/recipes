This recipe produces delicious, light, fluffy, and classic American pancakes that are quick and easy to make from scratch for breakfast.
<!-- more -->
1. In a large bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
2. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients and pour in the milk, egg, and melted butter.
3. Mix gently until smooth and well incorporated.
4. Heat a lightly oiled or buttered griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
5. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake.
6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges appear set, then flip and cook until golden brown on both sides.
7. Serve hot with butter and maple syrup or your favorite toppings.

Source: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/21014/good-old-fashioned-pancakes/
:::

::: slot ingredients
- All-purpose flour -- 1 1/2 cups
- Baking powder -- 3 1/2 tsp
- Salt -- 1 tsp
- White sugar -- 1 tbsp
- Milk -- 1 1/4 cups
- Egg -- 1 large
- Butter (melted) -- 3 tbsp
- (If using sourdough discard 1/2 cup: flour 1 1/4 cup, milk 1 cup)
:::
