::: slot default
A comforting, deeply flavorful Chinese chicken congee (jook) made with a homemade chicken broth infused with aromatic spices.
<!-- more -->
1. Make the spice package: Add coriander seeds and fennel seeds to the center of a cheesecloth, tie the corners together, and set aside.
2. Add the whole chicken to a large soup pot and cover with water just enough to submerge it. Bring to a boil.
3. Lower to a simmer and add the dried shrimp, yellow onions, spice package, and canned low-sodium chicken broth. Simmer for 30–40 minutes until cooked through, skimming off any impurities on top.
4. Remove chicken and let cool for 10 minutes (keep simmering the broth). Once cool, shred the meat and set aside. Return the chicken bones back into the pot.
5. Add salt and sugar to the broth and simmer for another 1.5 hours.
6. Meanwhile, rinse the jasmine rice thoroughly and let it sit at room temperature for at least 30 minutes to dry out completely.
7. After the broth has finished simmering, strain and taste for seasoning (add more salt if needed).
8. In a separate pot, dry-sauté the dried rice, minced garlic, and garlic salt over low heat until the rice turns a light golden brown (do not use oil).
9. Pour 10 cups of the hot homemade chicken stock into the rice pot and bring to a boil.
10. Lower heat to a simmer and cook for 1 hour on medium-low, stirring occasionally to prevent the bottom from burning, until the rice is mushy and reaches a smooth congee texture.
11. Ladle the congee into bowls and top with shredded chicken, green onions, cilantro, fried shallots, chili oil, and black pepper.

:::

::: slot ingredients
- Whole chicken (or bone-in chicken thighs) -- 4 lbs
- Yellow onions -- 2
- Dried shrimp -- 1/2 cup
- Coriander seeds -- 1 tsp
- Fennel seeds -- 1 tsp
- Low-sodium chicken broth -- 2 cans (14.5 oz each)
- Salt -- 2 tsp
- Granulated sugar -- 1 tsp
- Jasmine rice -- 1.5 cups
- Garlic -- 4 cloves (minced)
- Garlic salt -- 1/4 tsp
- Green onions -- 2 stalks (minced)
- Cilantro -- 1/4 cup (chopped)
- Fried shallots -- 2 tbsp
- Chili oil -- 1 tbsp
- Black pepper -- to taste
:::
