A classic Japanese savory egg custard that is silky-smooth, delicate, and filled with seasonal delicious ingredients like chicken, shrimp, and shiitake mushrooms.
<!-- more -->
1. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces and marinate with soy sauce and sake. 
2. Shell and devein prawns. Slice shiitake mushrooms and cut Kamaboko (fish cake) into thin slices.
3. In a bowl, gently whisk the eggs using chopsticks. Avoid whisking vigorously so you do not aerate or create excess foam.
4. Add the room-temperature dashi stock, soy sauce, mirin, and salt to the beaten eggs. Stir gently to combine.
5. Strain the egg mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into a liquid measuring cup to ensure a silky-smooth texture.
6. Divide the chicken, prawns, shiitake mushrooms, and fish cake evenly among individual heatproof teacups or ramekins.
7. Pour the strained egg mixture over the ingredients into each cup, filling to about 80% full. Pop any surface bubbles using a toothpick.
8. Cover the cups with their lids or wrap securely with aluminum foil.
9. Place the cups in a steamer with pre-heated boiling water. (Optionally wrap the steamer lid with a dish towel to catch water condensation).
10. Steam over low-to-medium heat for about 12–15 minutes until set. Check doneness by inserting a toothpick into the center; clear broth should emerge.
11. Garnish with fresh Mitsuba leaves (or parsley) on top before serving hot.

::: slot ingredients
- Eggs (room temperature) -- 3 large 
- Dashi stock -- 1.5 cups (360 ml)
- Light soy sauce (Usukuchi) -- 1 tsp
- Mirin -- 1 tsp
- Salt -- 1/4 tsp
- Chicken thigh (cut into bite-size pieces) -- 50g
- Sake (for chicken marinade) -- 1/2 tsp
- Soy sauce (for chicken marinade) -- 1/2 tsp
- Prawns / Shrimp (peeled and deveined) -- 4 small 
- Shiitake mushrooms (thinly sliced) -- 2 caps 
- Kamaboko (Japanese fish cake) -- 4 slices
- Mitsuba / Japanese parsley (for garnish) -- a few leaves
:::
